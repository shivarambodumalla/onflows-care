# Part 13 — Notifications

## Objective

Tell people about things they would otherwise have to go looking for — and nothing else.

## Principle

A notification is a claim on someone's attention. The bar is: **would this person want to be interrupted for it?** Most system events fail that test and belong on the timeline instead.

The product notifies on three things:

1. A follow-up assigned to you has **escalated**
2. An appointment is booked with you as the clinician
3. A lead you own has **converted**

Bookings, check-ins and completed visits are *not* notified. They are visible where the work is.

## In-app

A tray in the top bar with an unread dot. Entries show title, body, relative time and a link to the record. Unread entries are tinted; opening one marks it read and navigates. *Mark all read* clears the badge.

Capped at 20 in the tray — a notification list long enough to scroll has already failed.

## Browser

Uses the Notification API, permission-gated, for escalations while the tab is backgrounded. Permission is requested **on first relevant event, with an explanation** — never on page load, which is the fastest way to get permanently denied.

## Email

Configurable per reminder rule and toggleable in Settings. **Simulated in this prototype** — no email is sent, and every surface that offers it says so.

## Future WhatsApp

The channel most likely to actually reach patients in this market, and the reason the channel abstraction exists at all. Rules can already select it and Settings can already toggle it; the send is not implemented.

Modelling it now is deliberate: retrofitting a channel concept later would mean touching every rule, reminder and setting.

## Honesty about simulation

Every channel except in-app is labelled *simulated* wherever it appears — in the rule editor, in Settings, and in the notification tray footer.

This is a deliberate product decision, not a caveat. A stakeholder who believes WhatsApp reminders are working will plan around a capability that does not exist, and will discover the gap at the worst possible moment. A prototype that quietly implies working integrations is worse than one that admits what it is.

## Business rules

- Notifications are per user; switching demo role switches the tray.
- Escalation notifications are generated by the follow-up engine's catch-up pass.
- Read state persists.
- No notification is generated for an action the user performed themselves.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An escalated follow-up notifies its assignee.
2. Opening a notification marks it read and navigates to the record.
3. *Mark all read* clears the unread indicator.
4. Switching demo user shows that user's notifications.
5. Email, SMS and WhatsApp are labelled simulated everywhere they appear.
6. Browser permission is requested with context, never on load.
7. Your own actions do not notify you.
