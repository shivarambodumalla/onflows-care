# Part 1 — Product Foundation

## Vision

A clinic should never lose a patient to forgetfulness.

ONFLOWS CARE is the operating system for a multi-branch outpatient clinic: one place where every enquiry, appointment, visit, follow-up and document lives on a single patient timeline, and where the work that keeps patients coming back is generated by the system rather than remembered by staff.

## Problem statement

Small and mid-sized clinics run on a stack of registers, WhatsApp threads, spreadsheets and one receptionist's memory. The failures that follow are predictable and expensive:

- **Patients disappear silently.** A patient told to come back in two weeks doesn't, nobody notices, and the clinic finds out months later when they turn up elsewhere.
- **Enquiries evaporate.** Someone calls, asks about a treatment, is told "we'll call you back" — and nobody does.
- **History is fragmented.** The doctor's notes are in one book, the scan is in a WhatsApp thread, the payment is in a ledger. Nobody can answer "what happened to this patient?" in one place.
- **Nothing is searchable.** Finding a patient means remembering the day they last came.
- **Branches don't share.** A patient seen at one location is a stranger at the other.
- **Owners fly blind.** No reliable view of conversion, retention, no-show rates or doctor load.

None of this is a technology problem. It is a *memory* problem. The product's job is to remove memory from the critical path.

## Goals

| # | Goal | How the product delivers it |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | No patient is lost after a visit | Recording a treatment automatically creates the follow-up; overdue follow-ups escalate |
| G2 | No enquiry goes uncontacted | Every lead carries a next-follow-up date and generates a recurring chase task |
| G3 | One patient, one timeline | Every mutation emits a timeline event; the patient record is the single source of truth |
| G4 | Anything is findable in seconds | One global index over patients, appointments, leads, tasks, documents and staff |
| G5 | Front-desk work takes minimal clicks | Drawers over the working list, keyboard-first, autosaved drafts |
| G6 | Owners can see the business | Reports on patients, doctors, appointments, follow-ups and conversion |
| G7 | Safe multi-role, multi-branch operation | One permission matrix, enforced consistently, with a full audit trail |

## Success metrics

Measured against a clinic's pre-adoption baseline.

**Primary**

- **Follow-up completion rate** ≥ 85% of generated follow-ups actioned within their escalation window.
- **Lapsed-patient rate** — patients with a recommended next visit who never returned — down 30% within two quarters.
- **Enquiry contact rate** ≥ 95% of enquiries contacted at least once within 48 hours.
- **Lead conversion rate** up 15% relative to baseline.

**Secondary**

- Time to register a walk-in and book them: **under 60 seconds**.
- Time to find any patient record: **under 10 seconds** from any screen.
- No-show rate reduced by 20% (via reminders).
- Weekly active use by 100% of reception and clinical staff — a system used partially is a system that lies.

**Guardrail metrics**

- Zero patient records with conflicting or duplicated timelines.
- Audit coverage: 100% of permission-sensitive mutations recorded.

## Personas

### Divya — Receptionist
Runs the front desk at one branch. Handles walk-ins, phone bookings, check-ins, payments and enquiry calls, usually with someone standing in front of her. **Needs:** speed above all, forgiving forms, never losing half-typed work, one search box that finds anyone. **Fails when:** the system needs more than a few clicks, or she has to remember to do something later.

### Dr. Anil — Doctor
Sees 12–20 patients a day. Wants the patient's history in front of him without hunting, and wants recording a visit to take under a minute. **Needs:** clinical history at a glance, fast treatment entry, prescriptions, and to set the next visit without thinking about who will chase it. **Fails when:** the software makes him an administrator.

### Sunita — Branch Manager
Accountable for one branch's numbers and staffing. **Needs:** today's load, overdue follow-ups, staff leave and blocked time, and branch-level reporting. **Fails when:** she can't see what is slipping until it has already slipped.

### Rajesh — Admin
Configures the system: treatments, reminder rules, branches, users. **Needs:** confidence that a rule change does what he expects, and an audit trail when something is questioned.

### Dr. Meera — Owner
Runs the business across branches. Looks at the product weekly, not hourly. **Needs:** conversion, retention, doctor performance and branch comparison — and the ability to drill from a number into the records behind it.

## Scope of this prototype

This repository is a **working prototype**, not the production system. It runs entirely in the browser on seeded demo data held in `localStorage`, so flows can be validated end-to-end before engineering starts.

Deliberately **simulated** and labelled as such in the interface: email, SMS and WhatsApp sending; document storage; payments; report export; authentication. Everything else — registration, booking, check-in, treatment recording, the follow-up engine, tasks, leads, timeline, audit, search and permissions — genuinely works against the local dataset.
